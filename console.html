Three Engineering Privacy Guarantees

We decoupled camera functionality from cloud and network layers to ensure children privacy at home:

Zero Cloud Video Sync

Camera feeds connect to a local neural pipeline that calculates joints and discards the visual frame instantly.

Physical Lens Shutter

An included manual sliding shutter mechanically blocks the lens when games are turned off.

Complete Offline Play

All bundled games run entirely without internet connection, ensuring children are not exposed to unmonitored online networks.
