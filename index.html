Vision & Mission

FATH: A Name Signifying Distinction

In Arabic, "Al-Fath" denotes the unique, distinguished individual without equal. From this root, the FATH initiative was born: an umbrella uniting edutainment projects to discover and nurture talent, transforming passive screen time into active learning, physical agility, and fair competition. We engineer our platforms in Arabic from day one, not as an afterthought or mere translation of foreign media.

Long-term Aspiration

Our Vision

To establish FATH as the premier Arab platform for play-based and competition-driven learning, actively shaping a confident generation: sharp in intellect, physically agile, and proud of their cultural identity and Arabic language.

Safety & Controls

Protecting Home Privacy with Local Processing

Fath Play processes motion coordinates inside the onboard chip directly, without uploading camera footage to cloud servers:

100% Local Processing

Camera video is never transmitted to external servers or saved to internal disk; skeletal coordinates are processed in RAM and discarded instantly.

Physical Lens Cover

A sliding mechanical privacy shutter covers the camera lens when not in use for complete peace of mind.

Parental Time Management

Set daily screen time limits, lock games with one tap, and require 20 minutes of educational games before unlocking entertainment.
