Parental Tools

Features Tailored for Families

Individual Profiles per Child

Independent profile for each child on the Fath Console, with separate game history and age-appropriate time limits.

App Locking and Game Selection

Show or restrict any game for a specific child with one tap without altering console settings.

Screen Time Scheduling

Set allowed play hours on weekdays and weekends with mandatory rest intervals.

Active and Educational Progress Reports

Track games played and active minutes calculated without collecting any visual recordings whatsoever.

Incentive Reward System

Grant additional play minutes or avatar customization gear when specific learning exercises are completed.

Purchase Approval

No new games can be bought or downloaded from the store without explicit parental approval sent to your phone.
