Round Rules

Four Rounds Decide the Match

Each match features 3 randomly drawn rounds. A round win awards 2 points; a draw gives 1 point to each contestant.

Round 1: Speed

The Fastest Round (10 Seconds)

Mental alertness & split-second buzzer action

  • Electronic buzzer hardware measures priority in milliseconds to determine who answers first.
  • Strict 10-second response countdown from the buzzer press, across 5 core questions.
  • Correct answer adds 1 point; wrong answer or silence deducts 1 point.
Round 2: Evaluation

Confidence & Risk Assessment

Risk management & point distribution

  • Multiple-choice question presenting 4 options (A, B, C, D).
  • Each contestant possesses 20 points to allocate across options according to their confidence within 30 seconds.
  • Only points placed on the correct answer are awarded and added to the score.
Round 3: Momentum

Tug-of-War Round

Consecutive accuracy & resilience

  • Direct open questions where each correct answer pulls the digital indicator toward your side.
  • Achieving 3 consecutive correct answers ends the round immediately with a decisive victory.
  • Round cap is 15 questions; the contestant with the indicator on their side wins upon conclusion.
Round 4: Climax

Decisive Treasure Round

Direct individual examination

  • Each contestant answers 5 dedicated questions without interruption from the opponent.
  • 10 seconds allotted per question independently.
  • Each correct answer equals 1 point; the higher total secures the round.

Participation Terms

  • Arrival 30 minutes before match time for technical calibration.
  • Full adherence to referee instructions and round regulations.
  • External communication devices or unauthorized aids are strictly prohibited.
  • Arabic is the official language for questions and contestant responses.

Referee Committee

  • Head Referee: General oversight and final resolution of appeals.
  • Technical Judge: Monitoring buzzer hardware and millisecond priority.
  • Content Judge: Verifying linguistic and academic accuracy of responses.

Infractions & Appeals

  • Unexcused tardiness results in a 2-point penalty deduction.
  • Confirmed cheating attempts result in immediate season disqualification.
  • Written appeals may be formally lodged within 24 hours of match conclusion.
