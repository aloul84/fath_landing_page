Architectural Privacy

No Image Recording and Zero Cloud Transmission

The processor converts body movement into numerical joint coordinates (X, Y) instantly. Video frames are discarded in the same cycle and never saved to storage or uploaded. A physical lens shutter is provided for manual closing.
